Participation at the opening of Mitutoyo Competence Center at DTU - Technical University of Denmark

Calibras was proud to participate in the official opening of the Mitutoyo Competence Center at the Technical University of Denmark (DTU) — an important milestone for the future of precision manufacturing, digital metrology, and industrial innovation in Europe.

Driving Innovation in Manufacturing

The opening of the Mitutoyo Competence Center marks another significant step toward strengthening collaboration between academia and industry. The new center provides an environment where researchers, manufacturers, students, and technology partners can explore the latest developments in precision measurement, quality control, and digital manufacturing.

For Calibras, this event represents an exciting opportunity to contribute to the future of intelligent manufacturing through AI-driven engineering solutions.

Why This Matters

Manufacturing is rapidly evolving with advances in:

  • Artificial Intelligence
  • Computer Vision
  • Digital Metrology
  • Smart Manufacturing
  • Industry 4.0
  • Data-driven Quality Assurance

The Mitutoyo Competence Center will become an important hub where these technologies can be tested, validated, and integrated into real industrial applications.

Industry Collaboration

The event gathered researchers, manufacturing companies, technology providers, and innovation leaders to discuss the future of digital manufacturing.

Live Demonstrations

Visitors experienced advanced precision measurement systems, coordinate measuring machines, optical inspection equipment, and automated quality control solutions.
